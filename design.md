# Design — Vikipat

A locked visual system for the Vikipat storefront, derived from the supplied company banner.

## Genre
Modern soft commerce: confident product imagery, spacious rounded surfaces and friendly clarity, with Vikipat blue carrying the brand.

## Macrostructure family
- Marketing pages: Asymmetric product collage with a rounded brand canvas and modular collection cards.
- Commerce pages: Catalogue with clear filters, strong product imagery and persistent quote-list actions.
- Content pages: Long Document with large blue page openings and concise white reading surfaces.

## Theme
Deep ink navy and Vikipat royal blue dominate. White keeps the catalogue clean. Cyan, green, orange, magenta and fluorescent yellow appear only as registration marks and functional highlights. Canonical OKLCH values live in `tokens.css`.

## Typography
- Display: Outfit, 500–700, normal
- Body: DM Sans, 400–700
- Display tracking: -0.03em

## Motion
Only transform and opacity. Product lift and quote-drawer movement. No ambient ticker or decorative motion. Reduced motion removes spatial movement.

## CTA voice
Short active labels in pill controls. Primary actions use white-on-blue or blue-on-white; WhatsApp actions say exactly what opens.

## Shared rules
All routes share the Vikipat wordmark, brand blue, CMYK registration dots, rounded photographic frames, the two-font stack, and visible yellow focus treatment. No invented metrics, testimonials or client logos.
